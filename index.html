This Privacy Policy explains how the Breath Check mobile application (the "App"), developed by Cute Games ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use it. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

In short: Breath Check does not ask you to create an account and does not collect, store or sell your personal information. The App shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect certain device information to serve those ads, as described below.

1. Entertainment purposes only

Breath Check is a fun app made for entertainment. It is not a medical or diagnostic tool and does not actually measure your breath. Results shown in the App are for fun only and should not be relied on for any health decision.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, the App:

  • does not require registration, login or an account;
  • does not access or record your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, location or files;
  • does not store any data about you on our servers (we do not operate any servers for the App).

The App is free and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob, a third-party service operated by Google LLC. To show ads and measure their performance, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and operating system information (for example model, OS version, language);
  • information about ad interactions (for example ad views and clicks) and diagnostic data.

This information is collected directly by Google and is handled according to Google's own policies. We do not receive or control it. You can learn more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and user privacy

4. Your choices about ads

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). You will still see ads, but they will be less tailored to your interests.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you may be asked for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

5. Permissions

The App only requests the Internet permission, which is needed to load and display ads.

6. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the App are limited to content suitable for general audiences. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help address it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect or store personal information, there is no personal data held by us to retain or delete. For data collected by Google through AdMob, please use the options described in Google's Privacy Policy or your device's ad settings.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to its processing. Since we do not hold your personal data, such requests related to advertising data should be directed to Google. You are always welcome to contact us with any privacy question.

We do not transmit or store your personal information ourselves. Communication between the App and Google's ad services uses secure (encrypted) connections provided by Google.
